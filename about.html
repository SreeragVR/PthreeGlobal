OUR STORY

Founded with a vision to improve the quality of life within the spaces we inhabit, P3Global was born out of a passion for both human health and environmental sustainability. Drawing on decades of scientic research and rsthand experience witnessing the profound impact of indoor environments on people's lives, our founders recognized a unique opportunity to make buildings worldwide healthier for all occupants—starting in the Middle East, where climate conditions and rapid urbanization presented a chance to create lasting change from the outset. Meanwhile, we understood the growing need to prioritize energy and water conservation, reduce carbon emission, and improve operational effciency to save costs, which are urgent and practical concerns from many building owners and operators.

With decades of expertise in technology, sustainability, and health, we set out to create smart, connected environments that prioritize both people and the planet. Through our state-of-the-art AI and IoT-powered information platforms, we've transformed static buildings into dynamicspaces, that continuously contribute to both government and global environmental and health goals in a unied way.
