Impact on PEOPLE

Today, buildings are more than just physical spaces. With humans spending 90% of their time indoors, they are environments that shape the lives of those who occupy them and play a pivotal role in our planet’s future.

Buildings are major consumers of energy and water, accounting for 40% of global energy use and 70% of water consumption, significantly driving up operational costs.
Buildings designed with occupant well-being in mind can command up to 7.7% higher rents per square foot,  increased tenant demand, higher retention rates, and a stronger overall market position due to their focus on health and sustainability.
